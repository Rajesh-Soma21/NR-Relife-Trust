About NR Relife
Foundation

NR ReLife Foundation is a dedicated rehabilitation and recovery center committed to helping individuals overcome addiction and mental health challenges. Our mission is to provide a safe, supportive, and structured environment where individuals can heal and truly rebuild their lives.

We believe recovery is a journey that requires holistic care. Our approach combines medical detox, therapy, counseling, and wellness programs to ensure a long-lasting, sustainable recovery — addressing root causes through personalised treatment plans.
