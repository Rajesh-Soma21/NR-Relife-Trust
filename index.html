Our Story

About NR Relife Trust®

Nagaraj N Founder & Director, NR Relife Trust®

NR Relife Trust® is a non-profit organisation dedicated to fostering a compassionate society. Our rehabilitation work began in 2017, when Founder Nagaraj N started serving individuals in recovery. The Trust was formally registered in 2025, bringing structure, legal recognition, and a broader mission to the work already underway.

As our mission evolved, we recognised the urgent need for animal welfare, leading to our Goshala initiative, and ahead, a dignified Old Age Home for our elders. Every step is guided by compassion, transparency, and lasting impact.

Our Goshala Vision

The NR Relife Goshala will be a full-fledged sanctuary where rescued, abandoned, and elderly cows live out their lives with peace, dignity, and expert care. Rooted in India's ancient reverence for cows, this initiative bridges compassion with sustainability.

Our facility will feature open, shaded grazing pastures, naturally ventilated shelters, an on-site veterinary clinic, and a dedicated organic fertiliser production unit — creating a self-sustaining ecosystem that benefits both animals and local farmers.

"A cow protected is a community nourished — in spirit, in soil, and in soul."

— Nagaraj N, Founder & Director
What the Goshala Will Offer
  • Rescue & Rehabilitation — Dedicated intake for abandoned, injured, and elderly cows from across Karnataka with full triage and recovery support.
  • On-Site Veterinary Clinic — Full-time vet, vaccination drives, dental care, and specialist partnerships for surgeries and critical treatment.
  • Organic Fertiliser Program — Natural compost and cow-dung based fertilisers distributed to local farmers, promoting chemical-free agriculture.
  • Adopt-a-Cow Sponsorship — Families and corporates sponsor a specific cow's daily care, receiving regular photo updates and a personalised adoption certificate.
  • Indigenous Breed Conservation — Special focus on Gir, Sahiwal, and Kankrej breeds — protecting India's genetic heritage from extinction.
  • Community Education — School visits, farmer workshops, and open days to foster respect for animals and traditional agricultural wisdom.

Our Elder Care Vision

The NR Relife Old Age Home will be a warm, dignified residence for senior citizens who need care, companionship, and medical support. We believe every elder deserves to live their final years surrounded by kindness, comfort, and community — not loneliness.

The campus will be designed with accessibility, safety, and warmth at its core — featuring landscaped gardens, a reading library, a prayer hall, a physiotherapy centre, and a fully equipped medical bay with round-the-clock nursing care.

60Residents
24/7Medical Care

"Those who gave their lives to build our world deserve to be held gently as they rest."

— Nagaraj N, Founder & Director
What the Home Will Provide
  • Comfortable Residential Units — Private and shared rooms with attached bathrooms, emergency call systems, climate control, and senior-friendly furnishings.
  • 24/7 Nursing & Medical Care — Resident nurses, visiting physicians, specialist consultations, ambulance access, and a fully stocked dispensary.
  • Physiotherapy & Wellness — Daily guided exercise, yoga therapy, physiotherapy sessions, and mental health counseling specifically designed for senior citizens.
  • Dementia & Palliative Care — Specialised units for memory care, end-of-life support, and emotional well-being programs for residents and families.
  • Nutritious Meals & Dietary Plans — Chef-prepared vegetarian meals, personalised dietary accommodations, and dining in a warm communal setting.
  • Recreation, Library & Prayer Hall — Art classes, music therapy, cultural events, a well-stocked library, and a peaceful multi-faith prayer room for daily spiritual practice.
